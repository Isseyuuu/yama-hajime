Mt. Fuji 2026

Can I arrive and climb Mt. Fuji without booking?

Published and updated: August 25, 2026

Do not travel to the trail entrance assuming you can enter immediately. In 2026, all four routes require payment and an online course. Entry may stop when the daily limit is reached, and entering from 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m. requires a mountain-hut booking.

Gear is checked at the Yoshida Route fifth station

You may not be allowed through the gate without warm clothing, separate jacket-and-trouser rainwear, and footwear suitable for climbing. A poncho or one-piece rainwear does not meet the separate rainwear requirement. This information has been confirmed for the Yoshida Route; check the official guidance for any other route.

The short answer

You can book and pay online in advance and receive a QR permit. Booking opens in May 2026. If you do not have a smartphone, you can complete the procedure at the fifth station on the day, but it takes about 30 minutes. Always check the official English website again before travelling.

Can missing gear stop me at the gate?

Yes, on the Yoshida Route. Gear is checked at the fifth station. You may not be allowed through the gate unless you have warm clothing, separate upper-and-lower rainwear, and footwear suitable for climbing. Completing the QR permit procedure does not replace this gear check.

The official list also requires a headlamp, backpack, water and high-calorie trail food, a rubbish bag, cash and 100-yen coins, among other items. See the Mt. Fuji gear guide for the full list.

This confirmed information is specific to the Yoshida Route. Do not assume that another route uses the same check; review the latest official instructions for your chosen route.

Complete these steps in order

  1. Choose your route and entry time. The rules cover the Yoshida, Fujinomiya, Subashiri and Gotemba routes.
  2. Book a mountain hut if your entry time requires it. A hut booking is compulsory when entering from 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m.
  3. Take the required online course. It covers environmental protection and responsible climbing.
  4. Book and pay online. You will receive a QR permit. Each route requires ¥4,000: it is called a “toll” on the Yoshida route and an “entry fee” on the other three routes.
  5. Check the official site shortly before departure. The rules stated here are for 2026. Weather, local conditions and instructions still require a separate, current decision.

Where do I book?

For the Yoshida route, start at the official English Mt. Fuji climbing website. It links to Asoview for booking, and the service supports English. Online booking and payment produce a QR permit.

If you have no smartphone, an on-site procedure is available at the fifth station and takes about 30 minutes. This does not remove the hut-booking requirement for restricted hours.

When can I enter?

Entering from 2:00 p.m. through 3:00 a.m. requires a confirmed mountain-hut booking. This rule is intended to restrict overnight “bullet climbing.” A QR permit by itself does not replace the hut booking.

In 2026, the Yoshida and Subashiri routes are open from July 1 through September 10. The Fujinomiya and Gotemba routes are open from July 10 through September 10. Rules and local conditions can change, so confirm the official website again before travelling.

What happens when the daily limit is reached?

Entry is restricted when 4,000 people have entered in one day. People with mountain-hut bookings may still enter after the limit is reached, except from 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m. Check same-day official notices rather than relying only on your booking screen.

What about gear, shops and getting there?

The entry procedure does not show that your clothing, equipment, fitness or plan suits the conditions. Requirements vary with the route, weather, current conditions and individual experience. Use current official guidance and be prepared to change your plan. The Yoshida Route also checks equipment at the fifth station; see what to wear and pack for Mt. Fuji.

Before leaving your accommodation

Keep your QR permit available, confirm your hut booking when required, and revisit the official English website. The 2026 rules may not apply in another year.
