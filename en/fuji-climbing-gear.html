Mt. Fuji gear 2026

What should I wear and pack to climb Mt. Fuji?

Published and updated: August 25, 2026

Gear for climbing Mt. Fuji is not only about comfort. In 2026, equipment is checked at the Yoshida Route fifth station, and missing items may prevent you from passing through the gate. Confirm the required items first, then prepare for the weather, your route and your own experience.

Prepare these items first for the Yoshida Route
  1. Warm clothing
  2. Separate upper-and-lower rainwear — a rain jacket and rain trousers, not a poncho or one-piece garment
  3. Footwear suitable for climbing

You may not be allowed through the gate if these items are missing. This confirmed information applies to the Yoshida Route; check official guidance for any other route.

Where is gear checked, and what is checked?

On the Yoshida Route, equipment is checked at the fifth station. The check covers warm clothing, separate upper-and-lower rainwear and footwear suitable for climbing. This is separate from online booking and payment, even if you have already received a QR permit.

The official information confirmed for this guide applies to the Yoshida Route. We cannot state that the Fujinomiya, Subashiri and Gotemba routes use the same process. Once you choose a route, check its latest instructions on the official English Mt. Fuji climbing website.

Check that your rainwear has separate upper and lower pieces

The Yoshida Route gear check requires separate upper-and-lower rainwear. A poncho or one-piece garment does not meet this requirement, so prepare both a rain jacket and rain trousers.

Having rainwear does not mean it is safe to continue in severe weather. Rain, wind, visibility and trail conditions change. Review the forecast and local instructions shortly before departure, and do not decide to start or continue based on equipment alone.

Do more than simply pack warm clothing

Warm clothing is also checked on the Yoshida Route. What suits your plan and the conditions depends on the weather, route, time of travel, health and experience. This guide does not prescribe a specific fabric or number of layers. Check current official guidance and remain able to change your plan if you become wet or cold.

Check whether your footwear is suitable for climbing

The Yoshida Route requires footwear suitable for climbing. Do not judge by appearance or price alone. Consider your route, likely trail conditions and fit, and seek advice from a specialist shop if needed. Avoid using new footwear for the first time on the climb; test how it feels while walking and how easily you can put it on and remove it.

Suitable footwear cannot guarantee that you will avoid a fall or injury. Reassess the trail surface and your physical condition on the day.

How should I choose other items?

The items covered by the gear check are not everything needed for a climb. Consider water, food, navigation, lighting, communication and first-aid supplies according to your route, the weather, local conditions and your experience. Do not assume one fixed quantity or specification suits everyone; consult official information and expert advice.

Climbing Mt. Fuji in 2026 also involves procedures beyond equipment. All four routes require an entry payment and online course. Opening dates and restricted entry hours are covered in the 2026 Mt. Fuji entry guide.

Can I pass the Yoshida Route gear check with a poncho?

The Yoshida Route requires separate upper-and-lower rainwear. Prepare a rain jacket and rain trousers rather than a poncho or one-piece garment, and check the official guidance before departure.

Does a QR permit let me pass the gear check?

The QR permit and gear check are separate. For the Yoshida Route, prepare warm clothing, separate upper-and-lower rainwear and footwear suitable for climbing.
